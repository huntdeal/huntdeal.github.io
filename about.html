About HuntDeal

HuntDeal is an independent Indian shopping-content website focused on useful deal research and buying education.

Our goal

Online shopping can make it difficult to distinguish a genuine saving from a marketing claim. HuntDeal aims to add context: what to check, what specifications matter, and when a lower price may not mean better value.

How we approach content

  • We aim to explain the reasoning behind shopping recommendations.
  • We avoid presenting unverified discounts as confirmed bargains.
  • We update or correct information when we discover that it has changed.
  • Commercial relationships are disclosed clearly.

Affiliate disclosure

HuntDeal may participate in affiliate programs. If you purchase through a qualifying affiliate link, HuntDeal may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.
