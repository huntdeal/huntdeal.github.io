Backpack Buying Guide

A good everyday backpack balances comfort, protection and organization.

Measure your laptop

Do not assume a bag labelled for a certain screen size will fit every laptop.

Check padding

Laptop protection and back-panel comfort are useful for commuting.

Think about weight

A heavy empty bag reduces how much useful load you can carry comfortably.

Check weather resistance

Water resistance is not automatically the same as waterproofing.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
