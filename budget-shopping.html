Budget Shopping Strategy

Good budgeting is about reducing unnecessary purchases, not simply finding the lowest sticker price.

Set a target budget

Decide what you can comfortably spend before browsing.

Write down must-have features

This makes it easier to reject attractive but unsuitable products.

Compare total cost

Include shipping, accessories and recurring costs where relevant.

Pause before checkout

A short cooling-off period can help distinguish a useful purchase from an impulse buy.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
