How to Compare Products Before Buying

A fair comparison starts by deciding what you need and comparing products against the same requirements.

Define the use case

Write down the main job the product must perform.

Choose meaningful specifications

Compare specifications that affect your use case instead of collecting impressive-sounding numbers.

Compare ownership value

Warranty, compatibility, durability and service can matter as much as purchase price.

Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves

This helps prevent paying extra for features you will rarely use.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
