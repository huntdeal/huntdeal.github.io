Understanding Coupons and Bank Offers

The final checkout price can differ significantly from the displayed product price.

Read eligibility rules

Some offers require a particular card, payment method, minimum order value or membership.

Calculate the actual saving

Do not assume offers stack unless their terms explicitly allow it.

Check exclusions

Specific brands, sellers or categories may be excluded.

Re-check at checkout

The checkout page is the practical point at which the final payable amount becomes clear.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
