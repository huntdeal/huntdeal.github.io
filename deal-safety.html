Deal Safety Checklist

Before purchasing from an online promotion, take a moment to verify the basics.

Check the retailer and seller

Make sure you understand who is actually selling the product.

Check the product page

Verify model, variant, warranty and included items.

Review payment and return terms

Understand return windows and restrictions before paying.

Avoid pressure tactics

A countdown timer or scarcity message is not proof that a purchase is necessary.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
