HuntDeal Editorial Policy

Our goal is useful shopping information rather than a large number of shallow promotional pages.

Deal claims

We distinguish between a researched shopping opportunity and an unverified promotion. A discount should not be described as a bargain merely because a retailer displays a large percentage reduction.

Updates

Time-sensitive information should include a date checked. If a material detail becomes outdated, we aim to update or remove the affected claim.

Affiliate links

Affiliate relationships do not determine our editorial conclusions. Commercial links should be clearly identified.

Corrections

Readers can report errors through the Contact page.
