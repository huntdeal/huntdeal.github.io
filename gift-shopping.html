Gift Shopping Without Overpaying

A thoughtful gift does not need to be the most expensive option.

Start with the recipient

Think about hobbies, routines and genuine needs.

Choose a useful category

Practical gifts can be easier to match to a person's preferences.

Set a firm budget

A clear limit helps avoid last-minute overspending.

Check returnability

When preferences are uncertain, a straightforward return policy can be valuable.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
