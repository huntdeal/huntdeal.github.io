Headphone Buying Guide

The right headphones depend on where and how you listen.

Choose the form factor

Over-ear, on-ear and true wireless earbuds each have different comfort and portability trade-offs.

Consider noise control

Active noise cancellation can be valuable for travel and noisy environments, but is not essential for everyone.

Check comfort

Weight, ear-pad shape, fit and clamping force can matter more than small specification differences.

Check calls and connectivity

Microphone performance, multipoint support and device compatibility can matter for work.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
