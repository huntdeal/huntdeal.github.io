Home Office Essentials

A productive workspace does not require buying every accessory marketed for an office.

Start with the chair and desk

Comfort and correct working height are foundational.

Improve lighting

Good ambient and task lighting can improve comfort.

Choose the right display setup

A suitable monitor or laptop arrangement can have a bigger impact than decorative accessories.

Add peripherals selectively

Keyboard, mouse, webcam and audio upgrades should solve a real problem.
