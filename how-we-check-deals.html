How We Check a Deal

A useful deal is more than a large discount number. HuntDeal's approach is to look at the product, price context, conditions and practical value before calling an offer worth investigating.

Start with the product

Check the exact model, size, variant and included accessories. A discount on a different variant may not be comparable.

Check the price context

Compare with credible recent observations when available. An inflated list price is not proof of a bargain.

Check the conditions

Look for coupons, payment requirements, membership restrictions, shipping charges and limited-time conditions.

Check whether it fits the buyer

A low price is not useful if the product lacks features or quality the buyer needs.

State uncertainty honestly

If a price, stock status or promotion cannot be verified, describe it as unverified rather than a confirmed deal.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
