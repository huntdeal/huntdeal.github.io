Keyboard and Mouse Buying Guide

Peripherals are easy to overlook, yet they affect everyday comfort.

Choose for the workload

Typing-heavy work, gaming and general use have different priorities.

Consider ergonomics

Shape, key feel, mouse size and hand position can affect comfort over long sessions.

Check connectivity

Bluetooth, wireless dongles and wired connections each have practical trade-offs.

Avoid paying for unused features

Programmable controls and lighting are useful to some buyers but unnecessary for others.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
