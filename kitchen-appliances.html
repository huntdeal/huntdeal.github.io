Kitchen Appliance Buying Guide

For appliances, price should be considered alongside capacity, power, cleaning and service.

Choose the right capacity

Larger is not always better if the appliance will occupy valuable space.

Check cleaning requirements

Removable parts and simple surfaces can reduce maintenance effort.

Compare controls and presets

Useful programs can save time, but extra modes are not automatically better.

Check warranty and service

After-sales support can be particularly important for larger appliances.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
