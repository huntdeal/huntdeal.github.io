When Is a Laptop Deal Actually Good?

Laptop value depends on the workload, not simply the processor name or discount percentage.

Match the workload

Office work, study, coding, creative work and gaming have different requirements.

Check memory and storage

Adequate RAM and fast storage can have a major effect on everyday usability.

Check the display

Resolution, brightness, colour coverage and refresh rate matter depending on the work.

Check upgradeability and warranty

A slightly more expensive machine can be better value if it is easier to maintain or has stronger support.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
