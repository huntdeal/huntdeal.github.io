Monitor Buying Guide

Monitor specifications should be selected around your computer, workload and desk.

Resolution

Higher resolution can improve workspace and image detail, provided the computer can drive it effectively.

Refresh rate

Higher refresh rates are useful for gaming and can also make general motion feel smoother.

Panel characteristics

Brightness, contrast, viewing angles and colour performance matter for different tasks.

Connectivity and ergonomics

Ports, stand adjustment and VESA compatibility can determine how easy a monitor is to use.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
