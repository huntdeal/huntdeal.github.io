Power Bank Buying Guide

Capacity is only one part of choosing a useful power bank.

Check capacity

Higher stated capacity does not mean every device receives that amount of usable energy.

Check output

USB-C Power Delivery and other charging standards can determine charging speed.

Count the ports

Choose a layout that matches the devices you normally carry.

Check travel requirements

Airline rules can apply to power banks, so verify current carrier requirements before flying.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
