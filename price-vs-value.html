Price vs Value

The cheapest option is not always the best value, and the most expensive option is not automatically the best.

Identify the useful life

A product used every day may justify spending more for comfort or durability.

Consider reliability

Repeated replacement can make a cheap product expensive over time.

Avoid feature inflation

Extra specifications only create value when they solve a real need.

Compare alternatives

A less popular model can sometimes offer the same core experience for less.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
