How to Read Product Reviews

Reviews are evidence, not a substitute for your own requirements.

Look for patterns

Repeated comments are more informative than a single extreme review.

Separate product and seller issues

A delivery problem may not indicate a product-quality problem.

Check review age

Older reviews may describe an earlier version or changed packaging.

Look for specific observations

Comments about battery life, fit, build quality or compatibility are more useful than vague praise.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
