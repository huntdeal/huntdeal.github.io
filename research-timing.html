Best Time to Research a Purchase

The best time to research a purchase is usually before the sale begins.

Research normal pricing first

Knowing typical prices makes later promotions easier to evaluate.

Create a shortlist

Decide which models meet your needs before discounts appear.

Watch for changes

A promotion matters only if it improves the value of a product you already wanted.

Do not wait indefinitely

If a product meets your needs at a sensible price, a theoretical perfect deal may not be worth delaying a purchase.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
