How to Spot a Fake Discount

Discount badges can be useful, but they are not enough to establish that you are saving money.

Look beyond the percentage

A large reduction from a high reference price may still be an ordinary market price.

Check the exact variant

Storage, size, colour, generation and bundle contents can change the comparison.

Watch for conditions

Coupon codes, bank offers and membership pricing may make the headline price unavailable to everyone.

Compare total cost

Delivery charges, accessories and required subscriptions can change the effective cost.

Read reviews critically

Look for recurring comments about durability, fit, compatibility and performance.

Editorial note: Product prices, availability and promotions can change. Verify current details with the retailer before purchasing.
